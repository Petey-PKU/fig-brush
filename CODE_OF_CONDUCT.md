# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in fig-brush welcoming and respectful for
everyone, regardless of background or identity. We will communicate with care,
assume good faith, and focus criticism on the work and its evidence.

## Expected behavior

- Use respectful, inclusive language.
- Give and receive actionable technical feedback.
- Credit prior work and respect licenses and data rights.
- Protect private research data and credentials.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and repair harm when possible.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private data,
sexualized attention, personal attacks, and other conduct that would make a
reasonable contributor feel unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through the
repository's private contact channel. Reports will be handled fairly and
confidentially where possible. The maintainer may remove comments, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

This document is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
